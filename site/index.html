SeliaSheets

Private Android notebooks

A quiet, offline-first paper workspace for stylus ink, ordered pages, text, images, clean shapes, math, and complete PDF export.

No account. No ads. No first-party analytics. Your notebooks stay on your device unless you explicitly export them. Image text recognition is on by default and can be disabled in Settings. Google ML Kit collects SDK diagnostics and usage metrics but does not receive notebook content or recognition input and output.
