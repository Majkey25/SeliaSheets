A notebook for handwritten and typed notes, ordered pages, images, imported PDFs, shapes, and arithmetic.

No account. No ads. No first-party analytics. Your notebooks stay on your device unless you explicitly export them. Image text recognition is on by default and can be disabled in Settings. Google ML Kit collects SDK diagnostics and usage metrics but does not receive notebook content or recognition input and output.

Source and releases

Recognition and arithmetic can make mistakes. Check important results and keep backups. See the current feature limits.

Publisher and support

Matěj Teplý, publishing as Majkey on Google Play and Majkey25 on GitHub · majkeylab@gmail.com. Support contributions are optional and do not unlock app features.

For an accessibility problem, email the affected page or app screen, your device, and the assistive technology you use. Do not send private notebook content unless it is necessary and you choose to share it.
